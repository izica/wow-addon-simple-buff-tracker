# Simple Buff Tracker 1.1.0

## Show conditions

Every buff now has a **Conditions** tab: it decides when the buff is on the
panel at all. All the conditions you set must be met; a buff whose conditions
are not met is hidden in every display mode.

- **Where to show:** Open World, Dungeon, Raid, Battleground. All are checked by
  default; uncheck the places where the buff is not needed (a flask only in
  raids and dungeons, for example).
- **Item in bags:** shown only while you carry this item: the food for Well Fed,
  the oil for your weapon, a flask.
- **Talent or spell learned:** shown only if you have learned it, such as a
  talent of your specialization. **Reverse** shows it only while it is not
  learned, for your other specialization. Shift-click a talent or drag it into
  the field.
- The tab says right now whether the buff is on the panel and, if not, why
  ("Crab Cake is not in your bags", "turned off for: Raid").
- The tab counts the conditions set: "Conditions (2)".

## Buff window

- The buff settings have two tabs at the top: **General** and **Conditions**.
  The chosen tab stays when you pick another buff, so the same condition goes
  to several buffs quickly.
- Buffs that are not on the panel right now are grey in the list; hover one to
  see why.
- **Add Buff** puts the cursor straight into the Buff field.
- A new buff without a name no longer shows on the panel as a question mark.
- The window stays open when you open your spellbook or talents. Escape still
  closes it.
- The Move Up and Move Down buttons are gone: drag buffs in the list to change
  their order.

## Panel

- **Warn Before Expiring** now works in both display modes: a buff about to run
  out glows with the effect you chose ("Glow: Missing or Expiring"). In the
  "Missing or expiring only" mode it also comes back onto the panel, as before.

## Other

- On the first login with no buffs tracked, one chat line tells you where to
  add them.
