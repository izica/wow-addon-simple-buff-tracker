# Simple Buff Tracker 1.2.0

## Equipped items

A buff can now be an item you wear instead of an aura: a trinket, a fishing
pole, a riding crop. Choose **Track: Equipped item** and name the item. It
counts as active while you wear it, and a click on its icon puts it on.

- The tooltip says "Equipped" or "Not equipped".
- From chat: `/sbt add equip <item>`.

## New show conditions

- **Only in zones:** pick the zones where the buff is needed. The picker opens
  the continents; hover one to see its zones and tick them. Dungeons and raids
  are in a submenu of their continent. The zone you are in is at the top. The
  picked zones show as badges under the picker; click one to remove it.
  Smaller places inside a zone count too: a buff picked for Elwynn Forest
  shows in Goldshire.
- **Combat:** show the buff only in combat, or only out of combat.

## Per-buff settings

On the **General** tab every buff can now differ from the panel:

- **Show on Panel:** As the panel, Always, or Only when missing or expiring.
- **Glow:** As the panel, When missing or expiring, When active, Always, or
  Never.
- **Glow Effect:** the panel's effect, or one of its own (pixel, autocast,
  proc, pulse). The panel's default effect is None, so pick one here if you
  want a buff to glow while it is active.

## Other

- The buff settings scroll when they don't fit in the window.
