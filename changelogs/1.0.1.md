# Simple Buff Tracker 1.0.1

## Panel

- **The panel stays where you put it.** After a relog the panel could grow the
  wrong way (to the left, or to both sides) depending on where it stood on the
  screen: the game restored it by a point of its own. Now the addon alone keeps
  the place, and the panel always grows the way you chose.
- **Anchor.** The panel is held by the middle of the edge the icons grow from:
  growing right, the middle of its left edge. Unlocked, it shows that anchor as
  an open padlock with an arrow the way the icons grow, outside the panel so it
  covers no icon. Your place from 1.0.0 is moved to the new anchor once.
- The unlocked panel no longer jumps while you drag it.

## Clicks

- **Right-click a buff** on the panel to open its settings. A buff is put back
  with the left click only.
- **Minimap button:** right-click locks or unlocks the panel; Shift + right-click
  switches the display mode (it was right-click).

## Settings

- **Options -> AddOns -> Simple Buff Tracker:** the addon's page with its icon,
  version and what it does, and a big button that opens the buff settings.
